who is callum?

Based in Vancouver, Callum Frost is a dynamic young performer who brings the discipline of theatre training and the energy of competitive athletics to every role.

Having developed his craft through many musical theatre productions, he combines strong character work with natural stage presence and an athlete's understanding of physicality and teamwork. As a dedicated rugby player, he brings the same commitment to excellence and collaborative spirit to his artistic work.

At 16, he possesses the versatility to authentically portray a wide range of young characters, with the performance skills developed through years of theatrical training. His stage experience has given him exceptional character development abilities and vocal control, making him equally suited for on-camera work and voice performance.

Located in the heart of Vancouver's thriving film and television industry, Callum loves bringing his unique combination of theatrical experience, athletic discipline, and authentic youth perspective to screen and voice projects.

representation

Callum is currently represented by Esther Cohen at The Characters Talent Agency.
